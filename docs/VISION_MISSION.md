# ECHOLOG — Vision & Mission
## D19 · Evidence Preservation Layer of MoonWitness

## Vision
A future in which every important digital source used by MoonWitness can be preserved as an inspectable original — identifiable, protected, periodically verifiable, portable across storage systems, and recoverable without depending on one operator or vendor.

## Mission
Build a focused preservation service that protects original bytes, records what the service actually did, verifies integrity over time, and makes recovery independently testable — while leaving meaning, identity, rights, consent and human judgment with their proper MoonWitness owners.

## Promise
**Preserve the Original. Verify the Bytes. Keep the Record Inspectable.**

## Strategic objectives
1. Original integrity: stable content identity, atomic publication and no silent overwrite.
2. Operational preservation: repeatable fixity checks, clear failure states and non-destructive quarantine.
3. Evidence transparency: versioned receipts and event records with precise claims and cryptographic verification where justified.
4. Portability: standards-aligned export/import and storage migration without losing identity.
5. Recoverability: off-host copies, restore drills and measured RPO/RTO.
6. Privacy: default-deny access and consistent private-witness protection.
7. Ecosystem fit: ARSY/D00 remains the single Hub and identity authority; consumers retain domain meaning and the four-field reference.

## Success
An approved consumer can deposit an original, store a four-field reference, later inspect the ingest receipt, run or inspect a fixity check, export a portable package, and restore it to a separate host with every byte verified. All claims are backed by exact test and operational evidence.

## Values
Accuracy over spectacle. Preservation over feature count. Interoperability over lock-in. Human responsibility over automated verdicts. Privacy over discoverability. Verifiable operations over green dashboard theater. Small, secure core over platform sprawl.

## Brand voice
Calm, precise, archival, technical, human. ECHOLOG should feel like a trustworthy preservation instrument, not a crypto product, surveillance dashboard, generic cloud drive, or AI oracle.
